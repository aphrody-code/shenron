**_Dragon Ball Super_** ([Japanese](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Japanese_language): ドラゴンボール超（スーパー）, [Hepburn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hepburn_romanization): _Doragon Bōru Sūpā_) is a Japanese [manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Manga) series written by [Akira Toriyama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_Toriyama) and illustrated by [Toyotarou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyotarou). Set during the [time frame](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ellipsis_(narrative_device)>) of Toriyama's original [_Dragon Ball_ manga](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_(manga)>), it follows the adventures of [Son Goku](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goku) and his friends during the ten-year timeskip after the defeat of [Majin Boo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Majin_Boo). After meeting the destructive deity [Beerus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beerus), and attaining the power of a god, Goku ends up traveling to other universes. It began serialization in [Shueisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shueisha)'s monthly [_shōnen_ manga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shōnen_manga) magazine _[V Jump](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/V_Jump)_ in June 2015, and is currently on hiatus following Toriyama's death in March 2024. [Viz Media](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Viz_Media) has licensed the series for release in North America.

Toriyama wrote the major plot points, while Toyotarou drew the manga and filled in details himself. Toriyama's plot was also adapted into an [anime television series](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Super_(TV_series)>) produced by [Toei Animation](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toei_Animation), which aired on [Fuji TV](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fuji_TV) in Japan from July 2015 to March 2018. Two animated films have also been released under the _Dragon Ball Super_ brand: _[Broly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Super:_Broly)_ (2018) and _[Super Hero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Super:_Super_Hero)_ (2022), and were themselves tied into the manga. A sequel to the anime, _Dragon Ball Super: The Galactic Patrol_, which adapts the manga's "Galactic Patrol Prisoner" story arc, has been announced.

## Plot summary

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Dragon_Ball_Super&action=edit&section=1)]

See also: [List of _Dragon Ball_ characters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Dragon_Ball_characters)

[Beerus](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Beerus), the God of Destruction, begins searching for a warrior known as the "Super Saiyan God" that he saw in a premonition. After using the Dragon Balls to learn that Super Saiyan God is a temporary transformation his race can achieve, Goku has his fellow Saiyans perform a ritual so that he can attain the deity-like form. His fight with Beerus ends undecided and the latter spares Earth.

Following the events of _[Dragon Ball Z: Resurrection 'F'](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Z:_Resurrection_'F')_ (2015),<sup about="#mwt38" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Films_3-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Transclusion mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;Films&quot;,&quot;group&quot;:&quot;lower-alpha&quot;},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-Films-3&quot;},&quot;parts&quot;:[{&quot;template&quot;:{&quot;target&quot;:{&quot;wt&quot;:&quot;efn&quot;,&quot;href&quot;:&quot;./Template:Efn&quot;},&quot;params&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:{&quot;wt&quot;:&quot;Films&quot;},&quot;1&quot;:{&quot;wt&quot;:&quot;Although the events of the film are not shown in the manga, they are briefly summarized at the beginning of this story arc.&quot;}},&quot;i&quot;:0}}]}"><a href="#cite_note-Films-3" data-mw-group="lower-alpha" id="mwQQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwQg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwQw">[</span>a<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwRA">]</span></span></a></sup> Goku and Vegeta have both achieved a form beyond Super Saiyan God, dubbed Super Saiyan Blue, and are training with [Whis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Whis), when [Champa](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Champa_(Dragon_Ball)>), the God of Destruction of [Universe 6](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universe_6_(Dragon_Ball)>) and Beerus' twin brother, proposes a tournament between inhabitants of his universe and Universe 7, the one where Goku and his friends reside, in order to determine which universe will take Universe 7's Earth and get to use the Super Dragon Balls, the original planet-size Dragon Balls scattered throughout both universes from which the Namekian ones were derived, which can grant any wish. Goku, Vegeta and [Piccolo](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piccolo_(Dragon_Ball)>) face opponents such as [Frost](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Frost_(Dragon_Ball)>), the Saiyan [Cabbe](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabbe) and the legendary assassin [Hit](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Hit_(Dragon_Ball)>), before winning the tournament. It draws the attention of [Zenō-sama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zenō-sama), the Lord of Everything who rules over all twelve universes, who wishes to watch another tournament involving the rest of the universes in the future.

[Future Trunks](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Future_Trunks) returns from his timeline, having fled from someone he calls [Goku Black](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goku_Black), a Goku evil doppelgänger who wants to annihilate all mortals on Earth in the name of justice. Goku Black is eventually identified as [Zamas](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Zamasu), a [Lord of Worlds](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaiō_(Dragon_Ball)>) in [Universe 10](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Universe_10_(Dragon_Ball)>) and apprentice [Lord of Lords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kaiō-shin), who killed his teacher, used the Super Dragon Balls to switch bodies with Goku and killed him, traveled in time to Future Trunks' timeline, and killed the remaining Lords of Lords which in turn killed the Gods of Destruction. The Zamas of Trunks' timeline, who used the Dragon Balls to make himself immortal, also appears and teams up with Goku Black. They eventually fuse into one immortal body they call God Zamas and everyone plans to flee to the main timeline out of futility. However, Goku summons the Zenō-sama of Trunks' timeline, who then destroys the universe and God Zamas, while everyone else flees to the main timeline. Goku then retrieves that Zenō-sama and brings him to the main timeline, while Future Trunks and [Future Mai](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Dragon_Ball_characters#Future_Mai) travels back to his, but before the Lord of Lords died so he can warn Beerus about Zamas.

Both Zenō-samas later hold the Tournament of Power (力の大会, _Chikara no Taikai_), where teams of ten fighters from eight of the twelve universes battle, with the defeated universes being erased from existence. Goku, Vegeta, Piccolo, [Son Gohan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Son_Gohan), [Kuririn](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kuririn), [Android 18](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Android_18), [Tenshinhan](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tenshinhan) and [Kame-Sennin](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kame-Sennin) are joined by [Android 17](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Android_17_(Dragon_Ball)>) and a temporarily revived [Freeza](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Freeza). They battle formidable warriors, such as female Saiyans [Caulifla and Kale](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Caulifla_and_Kale) and Pride Troopers [Toppo](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toppo_(Dragon_Ball)>) and [Jiren](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jiren_(Dragon_Ball)>). Goku attains a state known as Ultra Instinct -Sign-, allowing him to dodge attacks subconsciously. Android 17 wins the tournament for Universe 7 and is awarded with a wish from the Super Dragon Balls, which he uses to restore the erased universes. As a reward for helping in the tournament, Freeza is permanently revived by Whis.

Following the events of the film _[Dragon Ball Super: Broly](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Super:_Broly)_ (2018),<sup about="#mwt42" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Films_3-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Transclusion mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;Films&quot;,&quot;group&quot;:&quot;lower-alpha&quot;},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;html&quot;:&quot;&quot;},&quot;parts&quot;:[{&quot;template&quot;:{&quot;target&quot;:{&quot;wt&quot;:&quot;efn&quot;,&quot;href&quot;:&quot;./Template:Efn&quot;},&quot;params&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:{&quot;wt&quot;:&quot;Films&quot;}},&quot;i&quot;:0}}]}"><a href="#cite_note-Films-3" data-mw-group="lower-alpha" id="mwZQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwZg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwZw">[</span>a<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwaA">]</span></span></a></sup> Goku and Vegeta are inducted as temporary members of the Galactic Patrol and tasked with capturing an escaped fugitive named [Moro](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Moro_(Dragon_Ball)>), a millennia-old magical being who destroyed innumerous planets by absorbing their life energy, before his magic was sealed by the [Great Lord of Lords](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Great_Lord_of_Lords). Near defeat, Moro fuses with the Earth, threatening to self-destruct and destroy the galaxy. Vegeta uses his newly learned Forced Spirit Fission in reverse to give the energy of their friends and the god power of [Uub](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uub_(Dragon_Ball)>), the reincarnation of the evil side of Boo and the half who actually inherited the Great Lord of Lords' powers, to Goku, allowing him to defeat Moro and save the Earth.

Bounty hunter Granolah learns from the Heeters, a sibling-run organization that deals in bounties and assassinations, that Freeza is alive and vows to get revenge for the massacre of his people. Using the Dragon Balls on planet Cereal, he becomes the strongest lifeform in the universe at the cost of drastically reducing his lifespan. The Heeters manipulate Granolah and Goku and Vegeta into fighting each other, during which, Vegeta, who has learned the technique of the Gods of Destruction, gains a new form dubbed Ultra Ego. Granolah's foster father, the Namekian Monaito, reveals that it was a Saiyan named [Bardock](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bardock) who saved them from the attack on Cereal ordered by Freeza 40 years ago, and that Elec of the Heeters is the person who killed his mother. Meanwhile, Elec uses the Cerealian Dragon Balls to make his younger brother Gas the new strongest lifeform. Monaito gives Goku and Vegeta Bardock's Scouter, revealing an audio recording of the Saiyan defeating Gas. It is also revealed that the Cerealian Dragon Balls were used by Monaito to wish that Bardock's sons "thrive"; thus being why Goku and his older brother [Raditz](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Raditz) survived Freeza's extinction of the Saiyans. Granolah charges up a final attack, which shortens his life even more, to finish Gas, but intentionally does not kill him. Having aged rapidly due to the battle, Gas begins fighting Goku and Vegeta again. However, Freeza appears and quickly kills Gas and Elec, before briefly unveiling a new form he calls Black Freeza and departing. Whis arrives to take Goku and Vegeta back to Beerus' planet to train in order to catch up to Freeza, who is now stronger than them.

[Magenta](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Magenta_(Dragon_Ball)>), CEO of Red Pharmaceuticals and son of [Commander Red](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Commander_Red), recruits [Dr. Gero](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Gero)'s grandson and genius scientist [Dr. Hedo](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dr._Hedo) to help build Androids for the reformed [Red Ribbon Army](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Red_Ribbon_Army) and to seek revenge on Goku and his friends. Because Goku and Vegeta are off training under Beerus and Whis, Piccolo uses the Dragon Balls to awaken his own latent potential, with [Shenlong](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shenlong_(Dragon_Ball)>) throwing in a "bit extra" as a bonus, and forms a plan with [Pan](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Pan_(Dragon_Ball)>) to lure Gohan to the Red Ribbon Army's base. Bulma arrives with [Son Goten](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Son_Goten), Trunks, Kuririn and Android 18, just as the fight against the giant version of Cell begins. Gamma #2 sacrifices himself and severs Cell's left arm. When it appears as if Piccolo has been almost killed, Gohan attains a new transformation, later dubbed Beast, and successfully defeats Cell Max with Piccolo's help. After the final battle, a repentant Dr. Hedo and Gamma #1 are employed by Bulma.

## Production

[[edit](/w/index.php?title=Dragon_Ball_Super&action=edit&section=2)]

When the [_Dragon Ball Super_ anime](<https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Super_(TV_series)>) was first announced, [Akira Toriyama](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Akira_Toriyama) was reported to be credited for the "original story and character design concepts", in addition to his regular role as series creator.<sup about="#mwt47" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-ANN_4-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ANN&quot;},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-ANN-4&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-ANN-4" id="mwgw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwhA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwhQ">[</span>3<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwhg">]</span></span></a></sup> [Toyotarou](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Toyotarou), illustrator of the _Dragon Ball Super_ manga, which began serialization before the anime began airing, explained that he received the major plot points from Toriyama, before drawing the storyboard and filling in the details himself. He would then send the storyboards to Toriyama for review, who would edit the initial draft, making changes to dialogue and art, before sending it back to Toyotarou, who illustrated the final draft and sent it to [Shueisha](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shueisha) for publication.<sup about="#mwt50" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-5" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-5&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-5" id="mwig"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwiw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwjA">[</span>4<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwjQ">]</span></span></a></sup> In addition to new characters designed by Toriyama, other characters for the "Universe Survival arc" were designed by Toyotarou, and a few by both.<sup about="#mwt53" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-6" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-6&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-6" id="mwjg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwjw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwkA">[</span>5<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwkQ">]</span></span></a></sup>

Although the anime series ended in November 2018, the _Dragon Ball Super_ manga continued with original story arcs.<sup about="#mwt57" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-7" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-7&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-7" id="mwlA"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwlQ"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwlg">[</span>6<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwlw">]</span></span></a></sup> For the "Galactic Patrol Prisoner arc", Toyotarou wanted the enemy Moro to be truly evil, like [Piccolo Daimao](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Piccolo_Daimao) was in the original _Dragon Ball_. He utilized elements of [goats](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Goats), a common Western demon motif, for the character and made his clothes _[shinigami](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Shinigami)_-esque.<sup about="#mwt60" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Moro_8-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;Moro&quot;},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-Moro-8&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-Moro-8" id="mwnQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwng"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwnw">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwoA">]</span></span></a></sup> Because Jaco and the Galactic King have "classic alien"-like designs, Toyotarou wanted Merus to have a retro look as well and so gave him a silver [bob cut](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bob_cut). But he also gave the character _[bishōnen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bishōnen)_ elements because he wanted him to be popular.<sup about="#mwt61" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Moro_8-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;Moro&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-Moro-8" id="mwow"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwpA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwpQ">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwpg">]</span></span></a></sup> For the Yardratian race, who are mentioned but never appear in Toriyama's original _Dragon Ball_ manga, Toyotarou and his editor Victory Uchida decided to use both the design seen in _[Dragon Ball Z](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Z)_ and a design created by Toriyama.<sup about="#mwt62" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Moro_8-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;Moro&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-Moro-8" id="mwqg"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwqw"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwrA">[</span>7<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwrQ">]</span></span></a></sup>

The "Granolah the Survivor arc" came about when Toyotarou created Granolah after having proposed various ideas for characters and being unsure if he should use existing characters or create new ones.<sup about="#mwt65" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Granolah_9-0" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;Granolah&quot;},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-Granolah-9&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-Granolah-9" id="mwrw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwsA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwsQ">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwsg">]</span></span></a></sup> After settling on a new one connected to the Saiyans, he sent Granolah to his editor Victory Uchida. After the two brainstormed story ideas, he was then given the go-ahead to bring it to Toriyama, who came up with the plot proposal and added the group known as The Heeters.<sup about="#mwt66" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Granolah_9-1" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;Granolah&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-Granolah-9" id="mwsw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwtA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwtQ">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwtg">]</span></span></a></sup> It was Toriyama's decision to have the newly revealed backstory on the Namekian race. Toyotarou called it the "sort of huge decision" that he (Toyotarou) can not make.<sup about="#mwt67" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Granolah_9-2" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;Granolah&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-Granolah-9" id="mwtw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwuA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwuQ">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwug">]</span></span></a></sup> Because Granolah's race went extinct in the story 50 years prior, Toyotarou made his clothing old-fashioned in addition to adding [steampunk](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Steampunk) elements. After Toriyama reminded him that the Cerealians were not a warrior race but did enjoy fighting, Toyotarou imagined them as [sharpshooters](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Sharpshooter) and gave Granolah his unique goggles. The goggles double as a communicator because he is a "lone warrior" and Toriyama had previously told him that having two characters converse better conveys characters' inner thoughts than having them talk to themselves. This led to the creation of the character Oatmeel.<sup about="#mwt68" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Granolah_9-3" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;Granolah&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-Granolah-9" id="mwvQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwvg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwvw">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwwA">]</span></span></a></sup> Toyotarou's designs for The Heeters were rejected three or four times before he came up with the final version, while Toriyama designed the Sugarians after Toyotarou's first two designs were rejected.<sup about="#mwt69" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-Granolah_9-4" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;Granolah&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-Granolah-9" id="mwwQ"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwwg"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwww">[</span>8<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwxA">]</span></span></a></sup> Toriyama also designed the Namekian Monaito, whom he originally named "Slug" before it was changed to differentiate him from the character of the same name in the film _[Dragon Ball Z: Lord Slug](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Dragon_Ball_Z:_Lord_Slug)_ (1991).<sup about="#mwt72" class="mw-ref reference" id="cite_ref-10" rel="dc:references" typeof="mw:Extension/ref" data-mw="{&quot;name&quot;:&quot;ref&quot;,&quot;attrs&quot;:{},&quot;body&quot;:{&quot;id&quot;:&quot;mw-reference-text-cite_note-10&quot;}}"><a href="#cite_note-10" id="mwxw"><span class="mw-reflink-text" id="mwyA"><span class="cite-bracket" id="mwyQ">[</span>9<span class="cite-bracket" id="mwyg">]</span></span></a></sup>
