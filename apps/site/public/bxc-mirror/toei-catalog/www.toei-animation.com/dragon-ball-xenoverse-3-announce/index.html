A new world brought to life by Akira Toriyama awaits in Dragon Ball Xenoverse 3

Licensing / Merchandising

Toei Animation May. 11, 2026
Share on

Bandai Namco Entertainment Europe invites fans and players to enter a completely new DRAGON BALL world by Akira Toriyama in DRAGON BALL XENOVERSE 3.
Coming 10 years since the last game in the series, DRAGON BALL XENOVERSE 3 was teased earlier this year with a trailer giving a glimpse of a new setting called AGE 1000 and revealed at Dragon Ball Games Battle Hour 2026, the marquee event for DRAGON BALL fans all over the world.

DRAGON BALL XENOVERSE 3 continues the series’ legacy with a completely new DRAGON BALL experience, where players choose the hero they want to become in an unexplored DRAGON BALL world brought to life by the original author, Akira Toriyama.
As revealed in the announcement trailer for DRAGON BALL XENOVERSE 3, players will get to explore and uncover exciting new stories in West City, the vibrant new world set in AGE 1000. Guided into the ranks of the Great Saiyan Squad, you’ll fight alongside allies as events there begin to take shape.
The game combines an epic story-driven RPG experience that will take DRAGON BALL fans on an unforgettable, action-packed journey into this new universe and its cast of characters.

Bandai Namco will reveal many more exciting details about this new entry in the DRAGON BALL XENOVERSE series in the months to come.

DRAGON BALL XENOVERSE 3 is coming to PlayStation5, Xbox Series X, and PC in 2027.
